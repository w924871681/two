# Attribution and data-use conditions for the E4 extension

The frozen extension contains processed arrays and author-generated source/initial
model states required to run the five E4 temperature groups. Its synthetic assets
are generated by the paper authors. Existing license notices on code and templates
remain unchanged; this notice does not relicense the whole repository.

## UCI Appliances Energy Prediction (E4_uci_T1, T2, T3)

Luis Candanedo, V. Feldheim and D. Deramaix, *Data driven prediction models of energy
use of appliances in a low-energy house*, Energy and Buildings 140 (2017), 81–97.
Dataset: Candanedo (2017), UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VC8G ; provider record:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/374/appliances+energy+prediction .
The dataset is licensed under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
The supplied arrays were modified by target selection, source-only normalization,
role segmentation and the study's controlled fault procedures. The controlled
faults are experimental interventions, not provider-supplied field labels.
This attribution corrects the page range 392–409 in the historical base-bundle
data notice; the author-hosted article and provider record identify pages 81–97.
The original notice remains unchanged as provenance.

## ThermBuild (E4_thermbuild_BSE1, BSE2)

Matthias Kersken, Fabian Raisch, Markus Male and Benjamin Tischler, *ThermBuild:
Real-world and simulated thermal data from 960 residential multi-zone buildings
in Central Europe*, Fraunhofer Fordatis (2026), DOI
https://doi.org/10.24406/fordatis/445 ; full provider metadata:
https://fordatis.fraunhofer.de/handle/fordatis/486?locale=en&mode=full .
The dataset is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The measurement-derived
arrays and their research modifications retain attribution and share-alike terms.
They select BSE1/BSE2 measured data and use source-only normalization, bounded
causal input carry, missing-target filtering and fixed role schedules. Injected
offset/dropout conditions are study interventions, not observed fault labels.
Retain these notices and the applicable CC BY-SA terms when sharing derivatives.

The extension manifest records group/domain/role relationships for every added
asset. Existing attribution, code and environment descriptions are in the base
bundle and the main Supplement S5. No fresh optimization or scientific run was
performed while preparing this package.
