# Figure Captions

**Fig. 2. Overall lifecycle maintenance performance.** Horizontal bars show point estimates and thin error bars show 95% center-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals for the equal-weight aggregate of the five primary lifecycle conditions (stationary, gradual, abrupt, recurring, and mixed corruption at alpha=0.10). Harmful transitions and commit rates are percentages; seeds are averaged within computing center before bootstrap resampling. Val.-Commit denotes ValidationCommit.

**Fig. 3. Robustness to unreliable monitoring data.** Grouped bars show frozen RAVM-DT estimates for mixed, persistent-bias, and replay corruption at alpha in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}; thin error bars denote 95% center-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. Alpha levels are categorical groups, and no interpolation or trend fitting is applied. The horizontal line in panel (b) marks the 5% synthetic calibration criterion.

**Fig. 4. Adaptation under benign operating evolution.** (a) Cleveland comparison of lifecycle MSE for RAVM-DT, Always-FT, and Frozen across four categorical operating regimes. Light connectors span the three point estimates within each regime. (b) RAVM-DT commit rate and beneficial-commit recall shown as paired dumbbells. Error bars denote 95% center-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals.

**Fig. 5. Mechanism analysis on frozen development pools.** (a) Paired lifecycle-MSE effects of block-consensus candidate construction relative to SinglePath and trimmed aggregation relative to mean aggregation. Differences are complex variant minus simpler reference; positive values indicate higher MSE for the complex variant. (b) Balanced lifecycle MSE versus harmful-transition frequency under mixed alpha=0.10 corruption for recent-only, long-history, and bounded-history evidence; error bars denote 95% center-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. (c) One-hundred-percent decomposition of each clean/mixed commitment outcome into harmful committed, non-harmful committed, and retained shares.

**Fig. 6. Sensitivity and generality.** (a,b) Balanced lifecycle MSE and mixed harmful-transition frequency across the existing 16-cell kappa grid; color limits follow the observed range with a neutral margin, and the outlined cell is the frozen setting (kappa_rec=0, kappa_hist=0.5), not a claim of universal optimality. (c,d) Paired effects across the six tested architecture-horizon cells under clean and mixed conditions, respectively. Differences are RAVM-DT minus Always-FT. Positive delta MSE indicates higher lifecycle prediction error, whereas negative delta harmful-transition frequency indicates fewer harmful transitions. Error bars are paired 95% center-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals.

**Fig. 7. External production-trace evaluation.** Horizontal grouped bars show performance on the exact prior Alibaba 40-machine external-domain split under clean and mixed alpha=0.10 conditions; thin error bars denote 95% machine-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. Pale bars denote clean data and muted blue bars denote mixed corruption. The dashed 5% line in panel (b) is the synthetic-domain calibration criterion, not an Alibaba-calibrated threshold.
